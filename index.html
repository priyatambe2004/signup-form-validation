Questions

Is this form sending my data anywhere?

No. It is a practice project, so the form is checked in your browser and nothing is stored or sent.

What makes a strong password?

Length matters most. Use at least 12 characters and mix letters, numbers and symbols. A short phrase of unrelated words also works well.

Why does the form show errors next to each field?

It lets you see exactly what to fix without losing your place, and screen readers announce each message.
